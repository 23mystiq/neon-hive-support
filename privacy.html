Privacy Policy

Neon Hive · Last updated 27 September 2026

Neon Hive does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following locally, on your device only. None of it is ever sent anywhere, and it is not visible to the developer.

  • Your save slots — difficulty, progress, and lives.
  • High scores and the name you enter alongside them.
  • Settings, such as volume levels.
  • Whether you have started the game before, so the opening cinematic can be skipped on later runs.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup and no way to recover it.

Game Center

Neon Hive uses Apple's Game Center for its optional global leaderboard. If you choose to submit a score, Game Center handles that submission — the game itself never sees or stores your Apple Account details.

Game Center is Apple's service, and Apple's handling of that data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device settings; the game remains fully playable without it.

Children

Neon Hive collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Network access

The game does not connect to the internet, apart from Game Center when you use it. All artwork, music, and sound are bundled inside the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
