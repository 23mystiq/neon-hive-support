Support & Contact

Neon Hive is a maze shooter for iPhone — twenty levels of neon corridors, three side-scrolling gauntlets, and four bosses between you and the way out.

Common questions

My progress disappeared.
Progress is saved on your device, not in the cloud. Deleting the app deletes its save slots, and they cannot be recovered.

Can I replay the ending?
Yes — finish the campaign once and WATCH THE ENDING appears on the main menu. It is tied to the save slot that finished, so deleting that slot also removes it.

There is no sound.
Check that your device is not in silent mode, and that the in-game volume sliders under the gear icon are not turned down.

My score is not on the leaderboard.
Leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your device settings, then submit a score from the results screen.
